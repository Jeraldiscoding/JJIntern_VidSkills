# Build a swipe worth making

Use for JJIntern picture posts and manual-swipe carousels. Career salary/company posts default to this format after the owner's 24 October 2026 correction; earlier video wording does not require motion or auto-advance. Infer routine production choices from the current owner direction and existing project conventions.
Required inputs: audience/question, useful takeaway, selected concept, platform, evidence/assets, desired website action and output location.

## Workflow

1. Select an employer-specific idea before styling. Check the current brief and prior topics; avoid repeating a company/angle the owner has excluded. Write an honest cover promise, the fact that resolves it and the JJIntern action it should earn.
2. Verify changing employer facts in current primary sources and inspect the intended JJIntern destination. Write the complete swipe sequence: hook, decision value, useful connection to the site, one CTA. Give panel 2 immediate value and each later panel a specific new discovery; justify slide count by information.
3. Choose consistent dimensions accepted by the intended placement. Keep pictures short, clean and informative: one dominant fact, few supporting words and clear reading order. Choose panel count by useful information; remove repetition before shrinking type.
4. Put material qualifications beside their claims. For salary hooks, use defensible Singapore evidence for the same employer and role, with no overseas conversion or proxy. Distinguish published intake pay from reported benchmarks; follow the career playbook's source, sample-size and undisclosed-date rules. Use real approved photos for photo requests, actual product captures for demos, and authentic assets. Do not generate people by default.
5. Render numbered sRGB images; inspect each alone and the whole sequence at phone size. Check clipping, order, contrast, UI overlays and swipe indicator coverage in a platform preview.
6. Deliver numbered PNG images, caption, per-panel alt-text draft, source/claim manifest, and completed concept/asset/artifact/release review. Repeat the same website action in the last panel and caption. Record one test variable and use qualified website visits as the primary outcome for these career posts.

## Gates and missing evidence

Every swipe must add understanding or make the next verified action clear. The cover must not overpromise; no fabricated salary, vacancy, feature, logo or testimonial. Preserve qualification readability and private-information protection. Keep currency, pay basis, location and eligibility limitations on the relevant panel. Never conceal essential context to force a click, imply the employer/role is on JJIntern without checking, or say “link in bio” without verifying that destination. JJIntern helps discovery; the student applies.
If an essential photo/capture is missing, finish the copy, panel plan and precise asset request; hold the dependent panel. Label a text/product carousel accurately rather than presenting it as a photographed story. Keep phone/upload checks pending until actually performed. Publishing needs separate authorisation.
