# Build a swipe worth making

Use for JJIntern carousels; do not treat the result as video frames with automatic timing.
Required inputs: audience/question, useful takeaway, selected concept, platform, evidence/assets, desired action and output location.

## Workflow

1. Select the idea before styling. Write the cover's promise and the conclusion that resolves it; compare distinct mechanisms if no concept is selected.
2. Write the complete swipe sequence and exact copy. Give panel 2 immediate value and each later panel a specific new discovery. Justify slide count by information.
3. Choose consistent dimensions accepted by the intended placement. Vary composition around evidence, comparison and reading needs; preserve unmistakable reading order.
4. Put material qualifications beside their claims. Use real approved photos for photo requests, actual product captures for demos, and authentic assets. Do not generate people by default.
5. Render numbered sRGB images; inspect each alone and the whole sequence at phone size. Check clipping, order, contrast, UI overlays and swipe indicator coverage in a platform preview.
6. Deliver image sequence, caption, per-panel alt-text draft, source/claim manifest, and completed concept/asset/artifact/release review. Record one test variable.

## Gates and missing evidence

Every swipe must add understanding. The cover must not overpromise; no fabricated salary, vacancy, feature, logo or testimonial. Preserve qualification readability and private-information protection.
If an essential photo/capture is missing, finish the copy, panel plan and precise asset request; hold the dependent panel. Label a text/product carousel accurately rather than presenting it as a photographed story. Keep phone/upload checks pending until actually performed. Publishing needs separate authorisation.
