# C — Which tab was that company in?

Prepared 2026-10-17. Audience: students who have been browsing employer pages and lost the thread. Format: 14-second vertical video; optional manual carousel variant below. Direction: **the tab that got away**. Desired action: choose one company and search `JJIntern.com/companies`. This is a relatable editorial fiction, not a testimonial or an assertion that JJIntern imports browser tabs.

## Hooks and selected mechanism

1. **“Which tab was that company in?”** → one remembered company name becomes a real search and result. Selected for recognition and a visual puzzle.
2. “You remembered the company. Not the tab.” → same payoff, more explanation; use if the puzzle is unclear.
3. “My internship plan: ‘figure it out’.” → replace the vague plan with one real company search. A separate joke direction, not a misleading real-user quote.

Three concepts compared on idea/evidence/relevance/product/feasibility (0–2): tab puzzle **2/2/2/2/2**; fake group-chat screenshot **1/0/2/1/1**; motivational promise montage **0/0/1/0/2**. The puzzle wins on an immediate recognisable problem and a small honest resolution. Reject the chat because no genuine conversation/permission is available. Reject the montage because it has no verifiable payoff.

Visual plan: 0–3.2 seconds is a full-frame editorial tab strip of plain generic labels, never employer/browser logos or a fake JJIntern screen. At 3.2 seconds one legible name remains and cuts into a genuine product capture. The joke ends as soon as the product starts. Dark neutral field, light sans; blue only on the remembered-name underline. No glass, glow, arrows, characters, avatar or voiceover.
