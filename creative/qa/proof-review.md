- Primary agent visually inspected six source checkpoint frames at phone-equivalent scale plus the encoded 9-second frame. An independent agent applied the QA skill to the actual capture record/export, sampled the first export at 2 fps across its duration and inspected full-size frames. This is frame inspection, **not real-time playback or a physical-phone test**.

Check current roles separately.” from the first product reveal to the CTA, replacing it with the explicit vacancy limitation at 8.6–10.2 s. The crop shows one complete result row. Brief A specifies organic Instagram Reels. Revised samples were reviewed; remaining external checks stay pending.
- Editorial width assertions ran on every rendered frame; no editorial overflow was reported. Genuine product placeholder text has its native clipped field presentation; the company name, typed query, qualification and CTA are visible. Tiny nonessential product details are not treated as phone-legibility proof.
