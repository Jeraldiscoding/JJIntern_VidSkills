# Creative review and release gate

Review the idea at script stage and the actual artifact after render. Use 0=missing/fails, 1=partly works with named weakness, 2=clear and supported. **Do not add the score into a quality certificate or view prediction.** Record evidence and the repair per criterion. A high total cannot offset a hard failure.

| Criterion | A 2 needs |
|---|---|
| Idea | Interesting student problem/discovery even with effects removed. |
| Hook | Specific, immediately understandable promise at phone size. |
| Payoff | On-screen resolution worth the opening attention. |
| Shot value | Each shot adds understanding; no decorative filler. |
| Product | Clear JJIntern connection appropriate to the brief: verified action/result for a demonstration, or honestly scoped editorial brand story. A metaphor is not evidence of functionality. |
| Copy | Human wording, appropriate Singapore context, no manufactured urgency. |
| Design | Hierarchy, composition and negative space have jobs. |
| Motion | Reveals, connects, compares or demonstrates; stable reading holds. |
| Readability/accessibility | Real phone and muted comprehension checked; important text/contrast clear. |
| Claims | Each material fact linked to evidence and current as needed. |
| CTA / ending | An appropriate honest next step, or an intentional brand signoff when recognition is the brief. Do not penalise a requested brand-only ending solely for lacking an on-screen URL. |
| Distinctness | Mechanism/composition differs from recent posts or baseline examples. |

## Motion and sound review from owner feedback

Use the [current creative direction](../CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md) and latest accepted film, not an old revision's approval gate or path. Check the actual encoded sequence:

- Can the viewer follow the same object through the morph and receiving action? Check boundaries, contact, masks and occlusion for jumps or accidental replacement.
- Does the travel look fast enough while the subject stays readable? Judge foreground and background motion together. For revisions of the discovery film, verify its fixed speed-shot packet and centred convergence.
- Are transformation, travel and reading paced differently? Avoid both sluggish transition pauses and rushing every beat.
- Does the result settle before acknowledgment? For the current discovery film, Found must stay brief and the logo must not drift; no second standalone Found screen.
- Do music and effects support the action? Check cue timing and energy, not only audio presence or loudness. Do not equate a sound-enabled browser pass with subjective listening.
- Do all accents clear before unrelated copy overlaps them? Inspect the actual transition frames as well as the clean holds.

Exact timing and style choices apply to their selected concept. Score other stories against their intended action and reading needs, not a mandatory packet formula.

## Hard failures: stop release

- Invented feature, interface behaviour, employer affiliation, evidence, testimonial or result.
- Unreadable essential words; broken, clipped or hidden layout; critical UI under platform controls.
- Repetitive animated slide cards offered as finished product motion.
- Salary missing currency/period/base-total/status/year/source or material eligibility/context.
- Hook never resolved, or proof materially weaker than promise.
- Private information or credentials in capture, source asset or export.
- Incorrect destination, misleading listing availability, missing rights, fake logos.

If a premise fails, change it before effects. If evidence is missing, remove the dependent claim or mark the shot blocked; do not let a disclaimer justify publishing fabrication.

## Review record

For each criterion: score / observed evidence / repair or limitation. Then list hard-failure findings, checks performed and checks pending. Separate:

- **Concept:** selected / revise, with reason.
- **Assets:** available / missing, with exact requested file/capture.
- **Artifact:** rendered and inspected / not produced.
- **Release:** ready / conditional / blocked, with remaining checks and owner.

Agents may select concepts and make reversible local revisions autonomously. A pending real-phone or upload-preview check means release is conditional; it does not mean the local work must stop. Do not mark a check passed because it appears in a template.
