SIGNAL TO POSSIBILITY — original JJIntern score

Composition and synthesis
20.000 s; 48 kHz; stereo; 24-bit PCM; 120 BPM; 10 bars of 4/4.
D major, with major ninths and suspended added ninths rather than stock
triumphant orchestral chords. The hook rises in register as the picture gains
focus. Its fragments become a syncopated arpeggio, then a wider glass line.
The final dominant resolves at precisely 17.000 s into a Dmaj9 brand chord.

Every waveform was written in Python: FM/resonator mallets, additive detuned
pads, sine/partial bass, pitch-envelope kick, bandpassed-noise snare, metallic
oscillator/noise hats, tonal ticks and newly synthesized transition noise.
Stereo delay and diffusion are generated as numerical taps. No recordings,
samples, stock loops, external music generators or sampled impulse responses.

The groove is a swung, syncopated electronic/future-garage arrangement. It
builds from a sparse opening to drums at 6 s, a glass counterline at 10 s,
sixteenth-note details at 14 s, then leaves space for the identity at 17 s.
Noise transitions are short and restrained. The low end is centered; pads,
mallets, reflections and fine details occupy the stereo field. A light kick
duck, soft saturation, subsonic filtering and EBU R128 mastering control the
mix without erasing the arrangement's changes in density.

Discarded approaches
- A repetitive sine-beep loop: no development, little emotional arc.
- A maximal EDM drop: too much masking and an abrupt tonal mismatch.
- Generic trailer brass, vocal chops or a licensed beat: inappropriate to
  the quiet precision of the brand, and incompatible with no samples.
- A last-second hard stop: the score resolves and its final tail fades.

Iteration
1. Composed the 10-bar harmonic narrative and rising five-note signature.
2. Arranged distinct layers for 0 / 3 / 6 / 10 / 14 / 17 s visual cue points.
3. Synthesized the instruments and algorithmic stereo ambience.
4. Measured the premaster, then performed two-pass EBU R128 mastering.
5. Re-measured the delivered WAV. Exact numbers live in score-manifest.json.
   Integrated loudness: -14.13 LUFS. True peak: -1.00 dBTP. LRA: 3.2 LU.
