The direction
The film now explains JJIntern's recurring-discovery value. A student repeatedly checking career pages sees an example posting appear. JJIntern's scheduled thirty-minute check catches the information, a named data packet races toward the website, and the camera arrives at the genuine internship feed. The invitation is to spend less time checking career pages.

This replaces the search-only demonstration. Search is not animated as the main product action; the real, filtered feed is the endpoint of the posting's illustrated journey.

What was chosen
The posting is the recurring visual subject. It begins as a labelled editorial example, becomes a packet, travels along the data path, and resolves into the real product view. The fast camera movement has a destination. The clock is separate from transport, so speed of editing does not substitute for the thirty-minute check interval.

Dark JJIntern ink, off-white type and blue data paths hold the visual language together. Natural system sans and Instrument Serif italic400 are retained; no text is squeezed horizontally. The portrait layout stays inside a conservative house composition inset while allowing decorative flight trails beyond it. The actual screenshot is uniformly cropped/zoomed, with no fabricated row or notification.

What was discarded
The prior directory-search tutorial showed a feature without explaining the user's larger reason to care. Decorative rotating geometry lacked a useful destination. Generic tab chaos alone would repeat that problem; it now occupies only the opening. A field of opportunity pulses could imply unsupported real detection events or complete coverage. An instant-alert or never-miss guarantee was unnecessary to communicate recurring discovery and is not shown.

A fresh Microsoft announcement was also rejected after capture: the genuine Research Intern row displays1Sept. The film therefore labels the event Example posting and the data flight Example workflow. The actual proof headline was changed from New postings to Internships so the old row is not presented as newly published today.
