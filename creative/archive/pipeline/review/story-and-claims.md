# Pipeline revision: story and claims

Decision date: **17 October 2026**, Europe/Stockholm. This records concept and copy decisions before animation polish. It is not a certificate for an uninspected export.

## Problem and action

Singapore students repeatedly checking employer career pages worry that an internship might appear between visits. Show JJIntern taking on recurring discovery checks, so the viewer understands why the feed is useful before seeing a search field. Desired action: visit **JJIntern.com/feed**. Format: 20-second vertical TikTok film, 1080×1920; native 120 fps master and 60 fps upload copy requested by the owner. No publication requested.

The original search-only story had a visible action but no larger reason to care. The owner now supplies that reason: a 30-minute scraping cadence. Hook → mechanism → authentic product destination → relief must remain intelligible with effects and sound removed.

## Hook options and chosen spine

- **Selected:** “Still checking career pages?” promises an alternative to repeated checking and resolves with the recurring check mechanism and feed.
- “What if a role appears while you're away?” promises discovery; discarded because it can tip toward an absolute missed-opportunity guarantee.
- “New internship. Another tab?” promises consolidation; discarded because it repeats the earlier tab/search entry point.

## Claims and exclusions

Approved exact factual line: **“We check every 30 minutes.”** The qualitative benefit **“Spend less time checking.”** is an intended value proposition without a numeric saved-time claim. **“New postings. One feed.”** depends on a current authentic loaded feed capture, not the historical loading screenshot.

Do not use “Never miss an internship”, “Every internship”, “All companies”, “Instantly”, “The moment it drops”, “Real time”, an invented inbox/Telegram notification, or a fake new Microsoft row. Fast editing may express progress but cannot itself claim measured speed. No salary, deadline, acceptance, auto-apply or outcome promises.

Suggested publication caption, if later authorised: “JJIntern checks for internship postings every 30 minutes. Explore the feed at JJIntern.com/feed. Employer-posting and data-flight sequence illustrated; cadence confirmed by JJIntern's product owner, 17 October 2026.” Recheck the cadence and current product before publication.
