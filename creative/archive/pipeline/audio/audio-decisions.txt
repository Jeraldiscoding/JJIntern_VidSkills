Synthesis and mix
Every oscillator, FM strike, additive partial, percussion noise transient,
transition and stereo reflection is computed by the local Python source.
No samples, recordings, vocal chops, stock loops, external music generation
or sampled impulse responses. Low bass and kick are centered; sequencer
details move across the stereo field. Short computed reflections preserve
precision. Light sidechain ducking, subsonic filtering, soft saturation and
two-pass EBU R128 mastering control the mix. Exact final loudness and true
peak are recorded in score-manifest.json after every build.
