This revision answers the feedback about a stretched look, decorative spinning, TikTok format, higher frame rate and clarity.

What changed
The layout was rebuilt for native 1080×1920 portrait. Nothing was squeezed from the landscape file. Short stacked phrases use natural type proportions, larger supporting copy and a compact central composition, with room reserved for TikTok controls/captions.

The blue ribbon has a visible job. It begins as a compact knot, untangles into the actual search viewport, stays still while Microsoft is typed into the real JJIntern company directory, then becomes the action arrow beside JJIntern.com. The final invitation now holds as one ending instead of changing titles twice.

The actual product input/result makes the motion's payoff concrete. New logged-out captures were made on17October2026 at390×844CSS pixels, then recaptured at4× device scale for sharp query/result detail. The search window starts wide and moves into a uniform left detail crop before typing begins; it stays anchored during the demonstration. This is a photographic crop of genuine UI, with no reconstructed rows or substituted interface text. Header/card role counts are excluded. “Company directory. Check current roles separately.” stays beside the result.

The material ribbon is rendered at2160×3840 and downsampled to1080×1920 for cleaner edges. Typography is rendered at final resolution. The product images are downsampled from genuine4× captures rather than enlarged low-resolution screenshots.

Frame-rate delivery
The master contains2400 independently evaluated frames at120fps, exactly20seconds. The TikTok copy contains1200frames at60fps, encoded directly from alternate original PNG frames in the same render. There is no optical flow, frame duplication or intermediate-video transcoding. Both are H.264 High, CRF15, yuv420p, square pixels, native9:16, explicit BT.709 conversion/tags, fast-start MP4, AAC320kb/s stereo48kHz.

Official TikTok Content Posting API guidance lists23–60fps and recommendsMP4/H.264:
https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/en/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide/
That is specific to the API; this production does not claim120fps playback support in every TikTok app/upload route. The120fps master is included for higher-frame-rate playback and reuse; use the60fps file as the TikTok upload copy.

What was discarded
A centre crop of the old landscape composition retained wide spacing and small text. Keeping a rotating sculpture behind new headings still failed to give it a purpose. Morphing it into a redrawn JJ logo would invent an official asset. The first vertical pass left the product UI too small, allowed an opening caption to touch the knot, and let the wireframe-to-arrow transformation linger. Those versions were replaced.
