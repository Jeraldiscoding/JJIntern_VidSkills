# JJIntern 20-second motion showreel: independent concept review

Reviewed 17 October 2026, Europe/Stockholm. Scope: local 1920×1080, 60 fps brand film with original synthesized music. This is a motion-design résumé piece about JJIntern, not a timed product demonstration. Prepared before animation polish from the public-product evidence already recorded in this workspace; the parent production agent is performing the fresh public-site check.

## Audience, problem and action

Singapore university/polytechnic students exploring finance and technology internships. The creative hypothesis is fragmented discovery: many possible directions, difficulty choosing a concrete next search. The desired action is to visit JJIntern.com and explore. That problem is owner/site positioning, not measured user research. The 20-second landscape format is explicitly requested; it allows distinct compositional transformations and a readable final destination.

The post log has no previous published posts. The local baseline rough demonstrates genuine company-directory filtering. This piece changes the narrative mechanism, opening composition, pacing and product entry: it is abstract typographic brand motion, not that rough expanded into a slideshow.

## Selected direction and creative rationale

Select **A: Noise → trajectory**, with the following guardrails. Its motion has one semantic job: convert scattered directions into a coherent next exploration. Preserve visual causality: a point becomes a path; that path organizes type; the organized type frames the destination. A transition should leave behind a useful shape, alignment or phrase in the next shot. Avoid unrelated particle bursts between title screens.

Use an asymmetric opening, a genuinely spatial middle, a large typographic category beat and a quiet final hold. This creates contrast in scale and density rather than relying on more effects. Introduce JJIntern or its modest authentic mark by approximately three seconds. Make “internships” readable before the midpoint so the movie cannot be mistaken for a generic technology ident.

The dark background, off-white text and purposeful blue accent come from the real served CSS. “Trajectory” is a proposed marketing treatment, not an existing app visual. Instrument Serif normal/italic at weight 400 can provide one deliberate contrast on “move”; the primary language should retain the actual system sans family. Avoid fake bold serif weights and do not download a replacement sans merely for style.

The original 512×512 transparent logo stays secondary and modest. Preserve its aspect ratio, colour and silhouette; do not vector-trace, smooth, redraw or turn abstract geometry into a replacement JJ logo. Proposed clear space is at least one quarter of the visible mark width. A newly set “JJIntern” line is editorial type, not an official wordmark asset.

## Beat and motion guidance

Suggested semantic structure: 0–3s: directional noise and readable hook; 3–6s: the organizing line is found; 6–10s: finance/tech/internships becomes unmistakable; 10–16s: line, type and geometry resolve in a distinctive brand transformation; 16–20s: stable final CTA and brand. This is a timing guide, not an assertion that a particular render has been inspected.

Keep chaotic detail outside the focal reading zone. A readable word should have a real hold before it is scattered. Allow the final URL and CTA at least 2.5 seconds stable. A 60 fps export is a delivery requirement, not a substitute for coherent timing. Limit full-frame white/dark reversals; beat sync can be carried by shape and spacing changes without strobing.

Music should express the same transformation: restrained rhythmic tension, widening harmonic space, then a clear resolved chord. Because the user requested synthesis from scratch, oscillator-generated tones and algorithmic noise are appropriate; recorded Foley, sample libraries, downloaded loops and generated commercial tracks are outside that request. Keep the original synthesis source and a stem/instrument manifest. Transitions should align to musical phrases while preserving reading time.

## Concept readiness and test

**Concept: selected with explicit limits.** It is appropriate to this showreel request, but does not prove product functionality. **Assets: available** for the necessary logo, observed palette and public type; source-distribution rights need their normal final review. **Artifact: not reviewed in this note.** **Release: conditional** on actual export QA, with real-phone/upload-preview checks identified honestly if unperformed.

Proposed future one-variable test: compare a question opening against an immediate “Finance + tech internships” opening while keeping the rest of the film fixed. Hypothesis: the direct category opening improves unaided understanding of JJIntern’s purpose. Primary outcome: correct purpose identification in a short muted viewing test. Guardrail: destination recall and no inference of guaranteed placement. This is a proposed test, not a performed experiment.

## Render QA checklist

- [ ] Inspect the actual MP4 at full size and at a 360px-wide equivalent; watch once muted. Record physical-phone and upload-preview checks separately if unavailable.
- [ ] Hook resolves; “internships” is readable early; JJIntern/category relationship is clear without music.
- [ ] Every motion reveals, connects, compares or organizes. No repeated floating cards and no effect sequence without a narrative relationship.
- [ ] Essential words retain stable reading holds; final URL/CTA stays readable at least 2.5 seconds; no clipped glyphs or unexplained font substitutions.
- [ ] Actual logo silhouette/proportions/colour are intact, with modest raster size and clear space.
- [ ] Off-white carries essential text. Blue/surfaces/opacity pass composited contrast review for their actual sizes.
- [ ] No simulated private UI, live-job inference, employer endorsement, alert, offer, salary, urgency, coverage, time-saving or product-latency claim has entered the final frames.
- [ ] All motion remains legible without rapid high-contrast flashes; transitions do not cause accidental single-frame text or blank frames.
- [ ] MP4 is exactly 20 seconds with 1200 video frames, 1920×1080, constant 60 fps; H.264 high-quality video, suitable pixel format and compatible AAC audio.
- [ ] Audio source contains only synthesized oscillators/noise and algorithmic processing; no samples. Listen for clicks, clipping, abrupt truncation and a musical ending at 20 seconds; inspect peak/loudness.
- [ ] Inspect a contact sheet and transition-adjacent frames, then watch the complete encoded file. Record the actual inspections rather than marking this list passed by default.
- [ ] Keep the editable render source, synthesis source and provenance. Final report distinguishes decisions, rejected ideas and actual visual/audio iterations from unperformed alternatives; elapsed time is measured wall-clock time, not estimated effort.
