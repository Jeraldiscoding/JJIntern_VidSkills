Synthesis and mastering

noise pressure; falling-pitch/panned launch; and short numerical reflection
taps. No samples, recordings, stock loops, vocals, external music model or
sampled acoustic impulse response. Modest bass ducking keeps kick attacks
clear, and two-pass EBU R128 mastering preserves meaningful contrast.
