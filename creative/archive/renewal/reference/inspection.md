## Observations from actual frames

1. **3 s / film-03s.jpg:** sage anodized metal laptop sits low in an almost seamless sage studio. A broad top/left soft source defines a fine edge highlight; the contact shadow is present but soft. The surface has low-gloss roughness and grounded weight. A spare, slightly elevated three-quarter view exposes thickness without dramatic perspective.
2. **7 s / film-07s.jpg:** the opened product is seen close to tabletop height in profile. A hand touches the display. A restrained rainbow reflection on the desk carries through the opening/typing sequence; this is environmental illumination, not a glowing outline around every object. The screen light remains local.
3. **8–16 s / sequence-08-16s.jpg:** cuts connect real typing, extreme close-up abstract key wells, then actual typing again. The matte granular green field and colorful recessed wells are tactile and macro scale. Individual wells compress and release with typing. The camera drifts diagonally; it does not fly into a tunnel. The metaphor resolves back to the product.
4. **16–24 s / sequence-16-24s.jpg and film-19s.jpg:** a shallow, white field of fine flexible rods ripples. Finger approach/touch is intercut with an expanding bowl of colored fibers, then a real image gallery being manipulated. The action gives the abstract deformation an understandable cause. Close focus and soft background preserve depth without shrinking the subject away.
5. **27 s / film-27s.jpg:** an extreme macro lens-like ring uses restrained translucent pearl and spectral refraction. Much of the frame is an out-of-focus bright material field; the curved rim is the focal cue. Highlights are broad and luminous, edges remain smooth, not neon.
6. **31–39 s / sequence-31-39s.jpg, film-35s.jpg:** dense ordered plates first read like a compressed stack, then spread into a layered plane of translucent sheets. Differing edge colors and overlapping shadows reveal real thickness. The oblique camera move and changing overlaps do the work. Cutaways return to the trackpad and real multitasking screen, making the abstraction about interaction/performance rather than ornamental geometry.
7. **43 s / film-43s.jpg:** warm peach replaces sage while retaining the soft-light studio logic. Pearlescent ribbed ribbons flow across the top and bottom of a close product view, with gentle shading and clear occlusion. The product stays substantial and stable. Warm/cool material contrast creates richness without a saturated-blue object.

## Transferable direction for JJIntern

Use the reference’s relationship between **tactile metaphor and real product proof**, broad studio reflections, controlled camera drift, and consistent material rules. The relevant premium quality is physically coherent edge detail, focus and light; it does not require copying the Surface palette or its abstract objects.

For the posting-to-packet story: a warm off-white document-like surface can separate along meaningful text/data layers into an ivory/graphite folio or compact bundle with finely polished edges. Keep it a substantial object, camera locked in depth during the exact 1-second horizontal transit. Resolve its arriving plane straight into a similarly framed region of the authentic feed, so the transformation explains discovery rather than merely inserting a product screenshot. Keep blue small and purposeful, matching JJIntern’s identity. The 30-minute cadence should emerge from repeated measured intervals/material indexing rather than a floating fake clock notification.

## Limits

Frame sequences establish the cuts, visual action and camera change; they do not establish the production software or physical BRDF parameters. Sound has not been audited in this research pass. All soundtrack elements for JJIntern must be newly synthesized under the user’s brief, not derived from this film.
