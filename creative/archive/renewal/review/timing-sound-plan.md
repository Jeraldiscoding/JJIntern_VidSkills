### Frame rule for the one-second flight

The lateral motion function must be defined only on **t ∈ [4.500, 5.500]**, or native master frames **540 through endpoint 660**. Its endpoint samples are exactly one second apart. Frames 540–659 are the 120 delivered flight frames; frame 660 is the arrival state and the start of unfolding. At 60 fps the corresponding range is frames 270–329, with arrival at 330. Do not lengthen the flight with a hidden pre-acceleration or post-arrival slide. Fold locally before it, unfold locally after it. A monotonic position curve may ease briefly at its endpoints, but its maximum velocity should occur over the central section; size and depth remain controlled.

## Original composition and material sound

**120 BPM, 4/4, nine bars, 18.000 s.** One beat is 0.5 s, so the one-second flight occupies exactly two beats. The contemporary groove is sparse and physical: low, dry kick; short brush-like noise snare; asymmetric fine hats; warm, restrained electric bass synthesis; and slowly changing harmonic air. There is no repeating plucked ostinato or arpeggio. Sound supports material and directional movement, with the posting ding the only deliberately chiming event.

Every audio sample is calculated from oscillators, envelopes, filters, delay and seeded pseudorandom noise. Do not import sound files, use acoustic recordings, use stock or platform loops, or sample an existing synthesizer preset. Render new float PCM at 96 kHz, then high-quality resample to 48 kHz for MP4 delivery. Retain the deterministic synthesis source and intermediate uncompressed WAV after approval.

| Element | Synthesis and arrangement |
|---|---|
| Groove | A two-bar pattern repeats with purposeful omissions and variations. Kick on selected strong and syncopated beats; snare/brush on the halftime backbeat; fine hats mainly on eighths with a few displaced sixteenths. Use velocity variation, not random sloppy timing. The opening has space, folding builds definition, flight removes one backbeat for movement detail, real feed restores the pocket, and the closing resolves it. |
| Kick | Integrate a sine oscillator whose instantaneous frequency decays exponentially from roughly 140 Hz to 43 Hz over the first 60 ms. Add a very quiet filtered-noise attack under 8 ms. Amplitude decay approximately 180 ms; remove excess sub tail so typography and folds feel precise rather than bombastic. |
| Snare / brush | Deterministic noise through broad band-pass regions, short 70–110 ms envelopes and a faint damped mode around 180 Hz. Lower transient crest than a pop clap; no recorded handclap or room sample. |
| Hats / texture | High-pass seeded noise and a small bank of inharmonic oscillator modes, 15–45 ms envelopes, restrained stereo variation. Keep 3–6 kHz clear around the material ding and avoid harsh bright hiss on phone speakers. |
| Bass | Monophonic sine/triangle blend with a quiet second harmonic and low-pass saturation, D2/A1/C2/F2 vocabulary. Use a few held and syncopated notes that answer movement; no constant sixteenth-note ostinato. Leave subroom for the kick. |
| Harmonic bed | Very quiet band-limited triangle/sine clusters around a D-minor-add9 palette, opening toward a warmer B-flat/C colour after the real-feed arrival. Slow amplitude envelopes and gentle oscillator detuning, no rising cinema braam, no familiar melodic quotation. |
| Posting ding | A single small damped metal resonance, approximately 0.18 s into the film. Combine three or four quiet inharmonic sine modes, e.g. 1,180 / 2,790 / 4,360 Hz, with unequal 0.10–0.35 s decays and a 3–6 ms shaped onset. The result should feel like a delicate material contact, not a two-note notification or synthetic alert. Tune by listening after rendering. |
| Fold sounds | Narrow-band noise friction whose amplitude follows fold angular speed, plus a few damped modal micro-transients at the actual crease closures. Low level, close and dry. No paper recording, exaggerated crunch or generic whoosh library. |
| Lateral movement | Band-passed pseudorandom turbulence with a spectral centre that brightens gently with velocity. Equal-power stereo pan follows the packet from left to right for exactly 1.000 s. Keep a mono-compatible central component for phone playback. No sound that trails into an apparent second flight. |
| Arrival / closing | Short, low-level 90–220 Hz damped contact at feed arrival, then a soft noise release. Finish with a quiet harmonic resolution and natural tail. No oversized impact, logo sting cliché or reverb wash masking the CTA. |

Use ducking around the ding and creases rather than making every effect louder. Suggested initial mix target: approximately −15 LUFS integrated, true peaks at or below −1 dBTP, with the final decision made on headphones, small speakers and a phone after AAC encoding. This target is a production starting point, not a platform requirement. The entire story must remain understandable muted.
