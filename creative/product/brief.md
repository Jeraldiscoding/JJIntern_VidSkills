## What the product does

The owner describes JJIntern.com as an internship discovery and search organisation tool for Singapore university and polytechnic students. The public website presents finance and technology internships, a company directory, application organisation, and Telegram alerts. **This workspace started empty: no application source, tests, database, existing captures, or roadmap were available. No functionality is source-code verified.** Public website inspection provides a useful but narrower evidence base.

Students looking at software engineering, data, quant, investment banking, M&A, and sales & trading are owner-supplied audiences. The public site names finance/tech and several career tracks; **an M&A-specific filter has not been verified**.

## Problems and conversion

The useful problem is fragmented discovery: students repeatedly visit employer pages, lose promising roles, and forget application state. These are creative hypotheses grounded in the owner's brief and the site's positioning, not measured user-research findings. Do not claim a quantified time saving or guaranteed hiring improvement.

Primary conversion: **visit [the public internship feed](https://jjintern.com/feed) and explore relevant roles**. Measure qualified visits and meaningful exploration where analytics exists. For content that demonstrates an account feature, use account creation as the next action only after that feature has been tested. The homepage says free to get started; this does not establish that every feature is permanently free.

## Do not advertise yet

No roadmap or planned feature has been verified. “Planned” must never be used as a substitute for “unknown.” Hold claims about AI matching, resume scoring, auto-apply, interview coaching, acceptance rates, saved time, exclusive opportunities, guaranteed placement, or salary outcomes unless new evidence establishes them. These are prohibited unsupported examples, not inferred plans.

Do not promote these unverified signup-page assertions merely because the site's own copy contains them: a three-day Goldman application window; signup in 20 seconds; alerts the moment a role appears; or 40+ job boards. They need current, specific evidence from the responsible owner and/or employer. The **30-minute check cadence now has direct responsible-owner confirmation (P06, 17 October 2026)** and may be stated at that scope; it remains owner supplied rather than source-code or telemetry verified. This new evidence does not establish coverage, instant detection, ingestion speed or alert latency.

The homepage's employer cards explicitly say **illustrative roles, not live openings**; the application board is also illustrative. They can demonstrate the homepage design with the disclosure intact. They cannot prove a vacancy, an actual student's application, employer endorsement, or a real offer.
