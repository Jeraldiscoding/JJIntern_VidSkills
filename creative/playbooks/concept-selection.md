# Select the idea before polishing

Required input: audience, one problem, one desired action, platform, available evidence/assets, production time. If a feature cannot be verified, change the idea to use a verified journey; list the unavailable journey separately.

## One-page concept comparison

For each of three ideas write: opening words/image; unresolved question; proof available; change the viewer sees; payoff; connection to JJIntern; CTA; what makes this different from the last three posts. Describe silhouette and movement, not adjectives such as premium or cinematic.

Compare on idea interest, proof strength, student relevance, product connection and feasibility (0–2 each). Explain each score in one sentence. Select with a written reason; a lower-scoring idea can win on a reasoned tradeoff. The numbers support a decision, not an outcome forecast.

Reject an idea immediately if its attraction depends on an unverified salary, an invented feature, humiliation, urgency that cannot be proved, or a hook with no honest payoff. Rewrite the premise before adding effects.
