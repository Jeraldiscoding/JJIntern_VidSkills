# Manual-swipe carousel

Choose a carousel when the reader benefits from controlling comparison, returning to a checklist or studying requirements. A carousel is not a video exported at five timestamps.

**Inputs:** audience/question, one useful takeaway, verified claims, product assets if needed, platform, slide count justified by information, primary action. Read brand guide, copy guide, relevant claim records and current platform guidance.

## Swipe structure

Design each swipe as an answered question that opens the next. For a five-panel “A big number needs a unit” carousel:

| Panel | Exact editorial copy | Information gain / composition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A big pay number needs a unit. | Large “number” and “unit” on a shared baseline. No salary bait. |
| 2 | Monthly. Annualised. Whole placement. These mean different things. | Three aligned unit labels with plain rules. Reader learns the distinction. |
| 3 | Annualised pay is not your summer earnings. | One sentence, a large annotated “per year” label. No hypothetical employer pay. |
| 4 | Check: currency · period · base or total · source · programme year. | Saveable checklist with generous line spacing. Keep all fields readable. |
| 5 | Compare the same units. Save this for your next role. | Payoff and one primary CTA; JJIntern.com as small signature, no unverified salary tool. |

This is an executable educational example, not a claim that JJIntern lists compensation. For a product carousel, every panel must use actual verified captures and make one concrete discovery. Do not repeat the cover headline across all panels.

## Production

1. Select the concept before styling. Write the complete swipe sequence and exact copy.
2. Use one consistent canvas per carousel; house starting point 1080×1350 (4:5), subject to the current placement's accepted dimensions. Keep essential content ≥80 px from edges and away from the swipe/page indicator in an upload preview. These are composition choices, not official safe-zone dimensions.
3. Vary density intentionally: strong cover, comparison, close evidence crop, useful checklist, satisfying conclusion. Keep reading order unambiguous. Use 76–112 px headlines and 48–60 px body as starting values; qualifications ≥42 px. Test every panel at 360 px wide.
4. Export numbered sRGB PNG files (`01-cover.png`, etc.), a plain-text caption, per-panel alt-text draft and a source/claim manifest. A photo carousel requested as photos should contain genuine approved photos; do not substitute generated people. Text/product carousels should be described accurately as such.
5. Inspect the carousel in order and individually. No auto-advance timing, animated cursor or missing context that requires the previous panel to remain visible. Material qualifiers travel with the claim panel.
6. Complete the same claims/readability/privacy checks as video, then preview the actual platform crop and panel order. Record missing phone/upload checks as pending.

## QA and testing

Ask after each swipe: what does the reader now know that they did not know before? Reject a panel whose only job is filling a template. The cover promises the eventual takeaway accurately; panel 2 should provide value immediately. Test cover specificity or panel-2 framing, one variable at a time. Evaluate saves, qualified visits and observed swipe-depth metrics when the platform actually provides them; do not invent completion rates or attribute a save to a person.
