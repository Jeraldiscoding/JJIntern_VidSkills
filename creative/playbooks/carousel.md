# Manual-swipe carousel

Career salary/company posts use pictures in a manual-swipe carousel by default, following the owner's **24 October 2026** correction. Choose the sequence around the reader's questions and the intended JJIntern visit. A carousel is not a video exported at five timestamps.

**Inputs:** audience/question, one useful takeaway, verified claims, product assets if needed, platform, slide count justified by information, verified website destination/action. Read current owner direction, brand guide, copy guide, relevant claim records and current platform guidance. For employer discoveries, also use [career content](career-content.md).

## Swipe structure

Design each swipe as an answered question that makes the next useful action clearer. For a company-discovery post, a useful starting sequence is:

| Panel | Editorial job | Information gain / composition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employer + specific reason to care | A strong typographic cover; location/pay basis/intake context visible where material. |
| 2 | Show what makes this opportunity relevant | A sourced responsibility, programme detail or compensation explanation. Deliver the hook's answer here if not already on the cover. |
| 3 | Help the student assess fit | Eligibility, work focus or a consequential constraint. A comparison or clear hierarchy should support a real decision. |
| 4 | Connect that interest to discovery | Explain the next useful JJIntern action, showing a genuine destination/capture when it helps. Confirm company/role presence before implying it. |
| 5 | One website action | A clear reason to visit plus a verified, legible destination. Repeat that same action in the caption. |

This is a flexible sequence, not a five-panel quota: combine adjacent jobs when the evidence fits cleanly. Vary the composition with the information instead of repeating a heading-and-bullets template. A carousel about one company needs facts distinctive to that company; a generic pay-unit lesson is not a substitute. For product demonstrations, use actual verified captures. Do not repeat the cover headline across all panels.

Resolve the opening honestly before the CTA. A click should help the student continue discovery, not reveal a fact deliberately withheld from the post. Do not hide essential qualifications, invent a downloadable guide, gate a public answer, or imply that a particular role is available on JJIntern without evidence. Use the employer/source links in the caption or source record for attribution; keep the main action directed to JJIntern as requested.

## Production

1. Select the concept before styling. Write the complete swipe sequence and exact copy.
2. Use one consistent canvas per carousel; house starting point 1080×1350 (4:5), subject to the current placement's accepted dimensions. Keep essential content ≥80 px from edges and away from the swipe/page indicator in an upload preview. These are composition choices, not official safe-zone dimensions.
3. Vary density intentionally: strong cover, comparison, close evidence crop, useful checklist, satisfying conclusion. Keep reading order unambiguous. Use 76–112 px headlines and 48–60 px body as starting values; qualifications ≥42 px. Test every panel at 360 px wide.
4. Export numbered sRGB PNG files (`01-cover.png`, etc.), a plain-text caption, per-panel alt-text draft and a source/claim manifest with source URLs, dates and exact supported facts. “Pictures” can mean designed editorial panels; it does not require photographs or generated people. A specifically photographic brief should use genuine approved photos. Describe text/product carousels accurately.
5. Inspect the carousel in order and individually. No auto-advance timing, animated cursor or missing context that requires the previous panel to remain visible. Material qualifiers travel with the claim panel.
6. Complete the same claims/readability/privacy checks as video, then preview the actual platform crop and panel order. Record missing phone/upload checks as pending.

## QA and testing

Ask after each swipe: what useful fact or next action is clearer? Reject a panel whose only job is filling a template. The cover promises the eventual takeaway accurately; panel 2 should provide value immediately. Inspect every exported PNG at 360 px wide, including the CTA and material qualifications; report physical-phone/upload checks separately. Test cover specificity or CTA framing, one variable at a time. Prioritise qualified JJIntern visits; use saves and observed swipe depth as supporting signals when actually available. Do not invent completion rates or attribute a save to a person.
