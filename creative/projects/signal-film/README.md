## Creative brief and selected concept

Audience: Singapore university and polytechnic students looking for finance and technology internships. Problem: fragmented discovery across many employer tabs. Action: explore [JJIntern's feed](https://jjintern.com/feed).

The owner's request is an ambitious, clean, dynamic company-quality advertisement. Transfer the accepted reference's rapid connected transformations, deliberate reading holds, original intact mark and drum-led energy. Use a new mechanism: a single blue search path brings a field of scattered tabs into focus, reveals the real website, becomes a scheduled-check orbit and resolves into forward movement. The authored graphics represent discovery; they do not simulate real listings or measured product speed.
