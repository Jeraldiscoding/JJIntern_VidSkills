GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Getting started should be the easy part.

What is JJ Intern?

JJ Intern brings Singapore internships from company career pages into one searchable feed. You can filter by career track, save interesting roles, track your applications, and connect Telegram for alerts.

Do I need an account to browse?

You can browse internships and explore companies without an account. Create a free account when you’re ready to save roles, track applications, or set up your Telegram alerts.

Which roles can I find?

We focus on Singapore internships across finance and technology, including investment banking, sales and trading, quant, risk, consulting, software engineering, data, machine learning, infrastructure, and product. Available roles depend on what companies are currently hiring for.

How do Telegram alerts work?

Create an account, choose your career tracks in your profile, and connect your Telegram account. Alerts help you keep up with matching opportunities without repeatedly checking career pages. You can disconnect Telegram whenever you like.

Do I apply through JJ Intern?

You apply directly through the employer’s career page. JJ Intern helps you discover roles and keep your applications organised; the company handles the application and hiring process.
