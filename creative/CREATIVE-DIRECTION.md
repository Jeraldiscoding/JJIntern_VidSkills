# What JJIntern videos should feel like

Updated **19 October 2026**, Europe/London, from the owner's feedback in this conversation and the archived production requests. Read this before choosing a visual treatment or animating another JJIntern video. The latest owner instruction takes precedence over an older brief or revision.

The owner wants work with the craft of a motion designer's showreel: a clear idea, clean graphics, convincing transformations, deliberate pacing and music with energy. “Premium” should be visible in the transitions, composition and sound. Adding more effects or making every shot faster does not meet that standard.

## Current example to learn from

**[Watch revision 11 — discovery packet](projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/JJIntern-revision11-60fps.mp4).** The owner moved on to a posting caption after this revision, then asked for its lessons to become part of this folder. It is the current accepted creative reference for this concept. Earlier renders remain iteration history, not equivalent approved alternatives.

Use the [current film brief and timing](projects/discovery-packet/README.md), [reproducible source](projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/source/motion.html), [production notes](projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/production-notes.txt) and [QA record](projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/review/final-qa.json). This is a preference and execution reference; publication, audience response and conversion performance have not been established.

## What we want

| Element | Direction for future work |
|---|---|
| Idea and ambition | A concrete story with a visible change and an earned payoff. Bring the craft requested by “go all out” and “make a statement” to that story. Avoid a sequence of generic title slides. |
| Visual design | Clean, neat, readable compositions with a small number of strong elements. For this family of flat motion films, start from pale blue, white surfaces, dark ink and JJIntern blue. Keep the authentic mark intact. |
| Transformation | Show where the next shape comes from. A boundary can become a packet edge; letters can compact into data tracks. The same object should remain identifiable through the morph. |
| Pacing | Make transformations quick and decisive, give motion time to establish its effect, and slow down for reading. Review those beats separately. The owner rejected both sluggish morphing and a version where everything became too fast. |
| Sense of speed | Use strong directional background movement, growing streaks and airflow around a crisp foreground silhouette. Screen position and apparent speed are separate choices. A subject can stay fixed while its environment rushes past. |
| Handoff | Show the object actually entering or connecting to its destination. Maintain the gap, contact point and occlusion through the transition. A cut straight to the brand loses the action the owner wants to see. |
| Result | Settle the focal element before a short acknowledgment. Avoid incidental logo drift while the viewer reads. A single combined result can replace several repetitive result screens. |
| Music and effects | Use a contemporary groove with a strong drum rhythm and intentional accents. Sound should support the morph, speed, contact and result. The owner rejected the earlier music as “hotel lobby” and asked for cooler drums and effects. Keep the story understandable muted. |
| Copy | Short, clear and readable. JJIntern handles discovery; the student applies. Preserve that distinction. Do not extend a text hold simply to fill the runtime. |

“Like Apple ads” is the owner's shorthand for polish, clarity and controlled motion. It is not an instruction to copy an Apple film, asset or soundtrack, and no specific Apple film was inspected for this update.

## What the iteration history rules out

| Earlier direction or problem | Latest resolution |
|---|---|
| Realistic metal, black studio, reflections, browser/feed detail and later illustration studies | The selected film uses flat white shapes on pale blue, a plain Microsoft / SWE Internship label and the original JJIntern mark. Do not restore those abandoned studies when revising this film. |
| Slow compression, then making the whole film faster | A quick, connected morph followed by distinct motion and reading beats. “Faster” is not a global time multiplier. |
| Request for a brief packet pause, then an awkward stop before travel | Later feedback removed the awkward interruption. Revision 11 deliberately holds the foreground during the speed shot while the background continues moving; this is apparent travel, not a dead pause. |
| Packet traversing left to right, including the later two-pass whip | Superseded by the final request: fixed packet during the four-second speed shot, then a gentle approach into the receiver. |
| JJIntern suddenly appearing and sitting on screen during arrival | The receiver enters from the right and moves toward the centre while the packet approaches and enters it. |
| Direct transition from packet to logo | Visible entry through the receiving packet, followed by the intact mark. |
| Receiver/logo continuing to drift through the result | The receiver settles at centre before “Found.” The owner explicitly rejected the moving logo during that beat. |
| Long logo hold followed by another full-screen “Found.” | One short bubble above the stationary logo, with a repeated ding. No second standalone Found screen. |
| Passive background music | A drum-led groove with cues synchronized to the action. Loudness alone is not energy. |

See [rejected patterns](references/rejected.md) for the concrete failure checks. Original requests and earlier sources remain historical evidence of how the direction changed; they must not silently override the latest resolution.

## What belongs to this film, rather than every future video

Revision 11 is **18.000 seconds**, 1080×1920, with a native 120 fps master and a 60 fps delivery. Its morph lasts 0.55 seconds, fixed-packet speed shot four seconds, and Found beat 0.85 seconds. The score is 160 BPM. These are working settings for this execution, not universal duration, tempo or frame-rate rules.

The rectangular packet, Microsoft SWE Internship label, receiver, Found bubble, exact pixel paths and final text-only JJIntern signoff belong to this concept. Other videos can use genuine product demonstrations, editorial explainers, humour or useful carousels. Keep the level of craft; change the story mechanism. The ban on browser screenshots and extra labels was specific to the selected packet film, not a ban on real product evidence in other concepts.

For this film the payoff is exactly **“We handle the finding. / You focus on applying.”** An earlier comment saying “We handle the applying” was feedback about screen duration, not permission to invent an auto-apply feature. The flight and Found bubble are editorial discovery graphics, not proof of a live Microsoft vacancy, instant detection or measured product speed.

The caption can carry a destination even though this film ends with the brand name alone. The [caption supplied for TikTok](projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/tiktok-caption.txt) is saved with the example. No publication is recorded.

## Use this on the next brief

Read the [product evidence](product/brief.md), choose the [story direction](playbooks/directions.md), then name which lessons above apply. State the intended pace of transformation, movement, result and reading before building effects. For an already selected concept, continue from its latest accepted version instead of reopening approved style exploration.

Review a motion test for continuity, apparent speed, legibility and sound timing, then inspect the encoded result. Check that effects end when their job is done and that the result is stable. Technical validation, subjective listening, physical-phone playback and actual upload preview are separate checks; record only those performed. Do not turn preference acceptance into a claim that the film is published, proven to perform, or fully release-checked.
