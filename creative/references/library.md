## R01 — A real change, shown in the same viewport

- **Source:** [Linear design-refresh article](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh); [embedded MP4](https://webassets.linear.app/files/ornj730p/production/a8da43a8ecf9e2265c24ab1dc5260d252514c997.mp4).
- **Actually inspected:** downloaded the 9.042-second clip; inspected sampled frames across its full duration at approximately 2 frames/second plus larger stills. This was frame inspection, not real-time playback. `ffprobe`: 3456×2234, approximately 59.94 fps, video stream only; no audio stream. Fine easing and exact click frames were not assessed.
- **Opening hook:** no editorial hook; an application is already open. Useful demonstration, weak cold-feed opening.
- **Curiosity:** which visible details change when the developer toggles the refresh?
- **Sequence/timing:** one continuous fixed-view screen recording, 0–9.04 s. Initial interface, visible switch to updated tabs/sidebar/properties, repeated comparison, then a settled interface. Timing is approximate from frame samples; do not copy an asserted frame-perfect edit.
- **Typography/composition:** UI fills most of a desktop recording; small developer controls at bottom. Too dense at this scale for a vertical phone post.
- **Motion/attention:** actual interface changes make the comparison legible because the viewport stays fixed.
- **Payoff:** the changed layout can be compared directly with its prior state.
- **CTA:** none in the clip.
- **Adapt for JJIntern:** verified input → visible result, retaining spatial continuity. Crop to the relevant control/result; supply a specific hook and hold the result long enough to read.
- **Do not copy:** developer toolbar, private/internal account data, desktop-wide framing, unrelated product identity or unsupported comparison claims.

## R06 — JJIntern discovery film: motion with a readable result

- **Source:** [revision 11 video](../projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/JJIntern-revision11-60fps.mp4), [current direction and revision history](../projects/discovery-packet/README.md), [owner preferences](../CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md).
- **Status:** current accepted creative example, established by the conversation's move to a posting caption and request to incorporate its lessons. No publication or measured performance is recorded.
- **Inspection record:** native storyboards, decoded 60/120 fps frames, full decode checks and real-time browser playback muted/with audio enabled. See the [motion review](../projects/discovery-packet/revision-11/review/motion-review.txt). Subjective listening, physical-phone and upload-preview checks were not performed.
- **Story:** source identity → connected compression → apparent speed → visible receiving action → short Found acknowledgment → finding/applying payoff → brand signoff.
- **Composition and motion:** flat pale-blue/white/ink treatment, strong rectangular silhouette, original logo. Packet stays fixed during the four-second speed interval; the background supplies velocity. Receiver and packet converge before the logo stops at centre for the 0.85-second Found beat.
- **Sound:** original 160 BPM groove with stronger drums and synchronized source, morph, air, contact and repeated receipt ding. Source and provenance are preserved; this is not a subjective audio-quality certificate.
- **Transfer:** rapid transformation, sustained motion, clear contact and stillness for the result each have a different job. Sound is part of the choreography. Clean graphics can carry ambitious motion without adding realistic materials or UI clutter.
- **Boundary:** do not copy its full packet sequence into every post, treat its exact timings as global rules, or present editorial motion as real vacancy ingestion or instant detection.
