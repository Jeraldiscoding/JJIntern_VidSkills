# Rejected creative patterns

Owner: creative lead. Created 2026-10-17; actual iteration lessons added 2026-10-19.

The first table contains **synthetic diagnostic examples** from the initial setup, when no original campaign exports were available. They are not attributed to reviewed posts. The second table records actual owner feedback from the discovery-film iterations. Both are rejection guidance, not product claims.

| Diagnostic example | Exact failure | Revision that changes the idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s: “Unlock your future.” 3–7 s: “Discover opportunities.” 7–12 s: “Elevate your career.” Each line appears in the same translucent card over blue glow. | No defined audience problem, no interesting fact, no change in understanding, no earned payoff. Smooth entrance animation cannot repair that missing story. | Pick one real decision: “Can you do this internship during semester?” Show the verified programme dates, explain the clash/check, end with a useful next action. |
| Large “S$12.5k” → employer logo → “Your next internship?” → JJIntern end card. | Missing role, currency context, pay period, year and source. Implies an individual anecdote is a typical achievable result. A logo is not proof. | Obtain a first-party role source with compensation context; show exact role, eligibility and pay period. If unavailable, drop salary and build the opportunity story around a verified task or programme detail. |
| “Less searching. More success.” above a full desktop screenshot, floating in a tilted glass panel. Slow zoom for 10 seconds. | Tiny UI, no meaningful action, no explanation of how the product helps; “success” implies an unmeasured outcome. | Record one supported user input and its real result. Crop to the control and response, retain site context, use copy that describes the visible change. |
| Carousel: six panels with identical “3 tips” cards; the last says “Follow for more.” | Every swipe repackages the cover. No discovery, escalation or usable takeaway. | One question → common mistake → concrete example → improved version → quick checklist → relevant product action, each panel delivering new information. |
| “One internship nobody is talking about” → generic career advice, never names a role. | Hook is never resolved; exaggerated exclusivity has no evidence. | Name the real opportunity early, reveal why its work/eligibility is unusual, show the dated employer source, and give a practical next step. |
| Arrows orbit a blue sphere while five employer logos slide in. | Movement signals no causal relationship; logos imply association/availability that may not exist. | Show the actual criteria being compared or a real shortlist if that feature is verified. Use blue to indicate the one changing selection. Omit logos unless legitimately sourced and relevant. |

## Actual discovery-film feedback

Read the [current owner direction](../CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md) for scope and the [film's history](../projects/discovery-packet/README.md) for the latest version. These are creative preference lessons, not published audience results.

| Rejected execution | Apply this correction |
|---|---|
| Slow morphing, followed by a version where everything became too fast | Pace transformation, travel and reading separately. Do not globally shorten the edit when one beat needs more energy. |
| Awkward stop between the source morph and its next action | Maintain the same object's continuity. A deliberately fixed foreground with a moving environment can communicate travel; an unexplained dead pause cannot. |
| Weak background speed and airflow | Strengthen directional streaks and air resistance while keeping the packet crisp. Effects must reinforce the motion, not obscure it. |
| Left-to-right packet passes and a whip in this concept | The final request fixes the packet during the speed shot, then uses slower convergence into the receiver. These supersede the earlier travel directions. |
| Complicated receiver, a sudden logo transition, or a long stationary wait before capture | Use a clean receiving shape entering from the right, visible contact and physical entry. Preserve the original brand mark. |
| Drifting logo during Found, long acknowledgment and duplicate Found title | Settle at centre before a brief bubble and ding. Show the result once. |
| Sound described by the owner as “hotel lobby” | Build a contemporary groove with stronger drums and synchronized action cues; do not substitute higher volume for musical energy. |
| Restoring rejected metal/studio, browser/feed or illustration studies | Use the selected flat source/packet/logo treatment for revisions of this film. Other concepts may use genuine UI or different art direction when the brief calls for it. |

## Fast rejection exercise

Write the idea in one plain sentence with no style adjectives. Read the hook and payoff together. If the payoff does not resolve the hook, revise before storyboarding. Cover every decorative layer: if nothing useful or funny remains, effects are hiding the problem.

A screenshot can be excellent evidence; it becomes a weak execution when it is unreadable, unexplained or repeated without change. Glass, glow and camera moves must perform a named job in that particular shot. They are never the concept.
