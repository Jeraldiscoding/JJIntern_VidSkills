## Analysis and proposed adaptation

These are creative hypotheses based on the owner's examples, the sourced pay fact and the official structural guidance. They are not observations of inaccessible videos.

| Evidence or input | Interpretation to test | JJIntern adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Owner's example pairs a known employer with an unusually salient pay number | A specific company and amount may make the topic immediately relevant and create curiosity about the role | Put employer, role and qualified amount in the opening; spend the next beats explaining the source and one useful role detail |
| Owner wants best-tech-company content | A finite, useful list may give viewers a reason to continue and save | Define the audience/lens early; give each company a distinct supported reason; end with a readable recap |
| The identified YouTube title specifies list size, pay criterion and year | A precise list promise communicates its scope before the viewer knows the entries | Give a JJIntern list a clear lens and relevant date; do not copy the count of 25 into a short edit or imply its unseen content was assessed |
| “Best” can refer to pay, work, eligibility, learning or personal preference | An undefined ranking can attract attention but leave viewers with an unreliable comparison | Choose a clear criterion; use an editorial shortlist when an objective ordering is not supported |
| TikTok's own ad guidance describes hook/body/close and deliberate audio/editing | A direct promise followed by value and one next action is a reasonable structure to test | Short information-first opening, readable evidence beats, intentional drum-led sound and a concise CTA after the answer |
| No attributable example-post analytics were accessible | We cannot tell whether topic, execution, creator audience, distribution or timing drove the observed reach | Test one hook variable and record retention, saves and relevant visits only where available |

The new [career-content playbook](../playbooks/career-content.md) contains proposed 20–24-second salary and 22–28-second shortlist structures. Those timings are starting points for JJIntern production, not measurements from TikTok clips.

Prioritise the owner's exact links. Otherwise look for a relevant Singapore intern-pay original and a company-shortlist original; a public creator repost can be used if its platform and inspection limits remain explicit. Do not label an old US salary ranking a current Singapore trend.

For each reference record the original URL, creator, publication/check dates, duration and coverage. Capture first-frame words/image, time to the first useful answer, each information change, text hierarchy, pay/ranking qualifications, sound, payoff and CTA. Separate observed details, interpretation and the JJIntern adaptation. Use timestamps only when measured; text or a thumbnail is not playback evidence.

Record visible metrics with the platform, date and denominator; leave unavailable metrics unknown. A high public view count alone cannot establish retention, conversion, organic reach or causality. Borrow the mechanism, not the creator's script, footage, identity or soundtrack.
