# Platform export and UI guidance

Owner: production lead. **Official sources checked 2026-10-17.** Recheck when preparing a release and at least quarterly. Platform requirements differ between organic posts, paid placements and upload routes. A shared export is a starting point; preview each destination independently.

## Working masters — JJIntern choices, not universal platform requirements

- Vertical short: 1080×1920 (9:16), constant frame rate, MP4/H.264, AAC when audio exists. The initial proof used 30 fps. The [discovery film](../projects/discovery-packet/README.md) uses a 60 fps delivery from a requested native 120 fps master; this is a local production choice, not an official TikTok requirement. Choose rate and encoding for the brief, and inspect text and gradients after compression. Prefer source capture large enough to crop cleanly.
- Manual-swipe photo carousel: numbered PNG or high-quality JPEG panels, 1080×1350 (4:5), consistent dimensions; 5–7 panels is a creative starting point, not a platform maximum. Export separate images, not an automatic slideshow. Use a 1080×1080 alternative only when the intended route requires it.
- Keep text/logo placement adjustable per destination. Do not permanently bake a universal “safe rectangle” into all compositions.

## TikTok

**Verified paid placement:** [TikTok Auction In-Feed Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads), updated June 2026, recommends 9:16 with at least 540×960 for Non-Spark vertical video; MP4/MOV are supported. Its current Non-Spark limit is up to 10 minutes and at most 500 MB. These are **ad specs**, not a reason to make a long organic post.

**Safe areas:** that official page explicitly varies the safe zone with dimensions, caption length and additional formats. Use its Standard LTR overlay for Singapore; use its anchor variant if that ad has an anchor. The page warns device-specific live presentation can differ from preview. Preview the actual caption, CTA and extras. A frequently reposted fixed pixel box is not authoritative for every placement.

**Organic verification gap:** [TikTok camera tools](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/camera-tools) redirected to an inaccessible support route during this check. No current organic maximum length or official numeric organic safe area was independently confirmed. The 1080×1920 master above is our production choice. Test a draft in the account's current upload flow; check expanded captions and right-side controls.

## Instagram Reels

**Verified paid guidance:** [Meta Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) recommends vertical 9:16 creative, audio and key messages within its safe zone, and links to a Reels safe-zone checker. This is paid guidance, not an organic specification. Its comparative advertising studies do not predict JJIntern performance.

**Verification gaps:** the official [Reel size/aspect ratio help page](https://help.instagram.com/1038071743007909) returned HTTP 429; [Meta's Reels ad-spec route](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels) redirected to login/temporary block. Exact current organic limits, paid maximum length, recommended resolution and numeric Meta safe-zone percentages were therefore not verified here. Do not silently replace this gap with a third-party spec table.

**Production action:** use the vertical working master; obtain the current official placement overlay/checker in the authorized publishing flow. Preview the actual caption, profile/header, action controls and ad CTA. Check immersive playback, feed and profile-cover crops separately. Keep all necessary words readable in the actual Reel; the cover is not a substitute for its opening shot. No invented official pixel coordinates are supplied.

## Instagram photo carousels

Official [multiple-photo posting help](https://help.instagram.com/269314186824048) and [photo resolution help](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723) were unavailable through accessible help routes; current panel limits and all accepted ratios are **not verified** in this document. Paid carousel limits cannot be assumed to equal organic limits. Before upload, confirm panel count, dimensions and crop behavior in the intended account/tool.

Our 4:5, 5–7-panel working format deliberately stays simple. Preview every exported panel in order; check the panel-number badge and crop in feed/profile. Put substantial content inside a visible inset; judge that inset from preview, not the Reels overlay. Write alt text or an equivalent ordered caption where the chosen publishing route supports it. Source details must remain readable on a phone.

## YouTube Shorts

**Organic:** [YouTube's three-minute Shorts help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877) states that square or vertical uploads up to three minutes, uploaded on or after 2024-10-15 on standard channels, are classified as Shorts. Shorts over one minute with an active copyright claim are blocked globally. Track music permission and duration; availability in one platform's library does not establish cross-platform rights.

**Paid:** [Google's Shorts ad guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697) recommends vertical assets and videos under 60 seconds; for longer eligible ads only the first 60 seconds plays in the Shorts feed, with continuation behavior described there. Campaign type matters; do not confuse this with organic Shorts' three-minute eligibility.

**Safe areas:** [Google's official Shorts ads page](https://business.google.com/us/ad-solutions/youtube-ads/shorts-ads/) specifies keeping critical elements away from the top 10%, bottom 25% and right 10%. On a 1080×1920 frame these reservations correspond to top 192 px, bottom 480 px and right 108 px. This is the cited **Shorts ad page's guidance**, not a cross-platform or organic guarantee; other Google campaign templates can be more conservative. Inspect the actual CTA overlay/caption/control layout and intended placement before release. For organic Shorts, preview the actual account/app UI instead of labeling those ad numbers an official organic rule.

## Apply safe areas to the actual edit

1. Record platform, organic/paid, upload route and intended caption/CTA in the brief.
2. Obtain the current official placement overlay when accessible; retain its source/date with the project. If unavailable, mark that verification pending.
3. Put the export in the actual draft/preview interface using a representative phone. Do not publish as a test.
4. Inspect opening text, role/employer, pay qualifications, source/date, product action and CTA with controls/captions visible. Move or resize the layout where any necessary content overlaps UI.
5. Inspect without sound and at normal playback speed; check source qualifiers are readable during their real dwell. Correct layout and timing before final export.

No automated overlay can certify every app version, screen, caption length or placement. Final phone/draft checks remain an explicit QA item, with any unperformed check marked pending.
